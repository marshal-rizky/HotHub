Why our number differs from official figures

One fixed threshold Aqua MODIS, confidence 30 or higher. VIIRS months are converted to that unit.
Satellites never added together Each one is kept apart, so a new launch cannot inflate the count.
Always-hot places left out Gas flares, smelters and volcanoes are hot every day and are not fires.
Agencies disagree August 2026, high confidence: 9,409 (SiPongi+) and 9,587 (BRIN). Source: Kompas, 7 September 2026.

Methods

Data. NASA GIBS: VIIRS (Suomi NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21) and MODIS (Aqua, Terra) Thermal Anomalies; Blue Marble Shaded Relief and Bathymetry (base map). Meteomatics: aerosol optical depth at 550 nm (smoke flag).

Code and design. three.js (MIT licence) for the map. Colour steps from ColorBrewer (OrRd) by Cynthia Brewer. System fonts only.

Other figures. Damage and burned-area figures for 2015 and 2019: World Bank, The Cost of Fire (2016), and Indonesia Economic Quarterly, December 2019. City and island sizes are rounded.

AI use. Code and documents were written with the help of an AI coding assistant (Claude, through Claude Code), directed and reviewed by the team. The logo, the small pixel icons and the comic panels are grids drawn in code with that assistant; no image on this page comes from an image generator.

Hot spots are heat anomalies seen by satellite, not confirmed fires. This page reports what the sensors saw; it does not say who lit a fire or why. Source repository
